## Why This One Works

The boxed skillet dinners promise this exact plate and charge for the box, the seasoning packet and the name. This version buys the parts instead: ground beef browned hard for flavour, macaroni that cooks right in the tomato sauce and drinks it up as it goes, and real cheese melted over the top at the end. It lands in Tier 3 because beef and cheese are the two priciest lines in the pan, and the page says so plainly rather than shrinking the portions to chase a lower tier. The yield was measured, not guessed: about 2.4 litres of skillet, six plates of roughly 400 ml each.

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Heat the oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef and the onion and cook for about 8 minutes, breaking the beef up with a spoon, until the beef is well browned and the onion has softened. Drain off any extra fat if the beef gave up a lot.</li>
  <li>Add the garlic, paprika and oregano and stir for 1 minute, until the spices smell warm.</li>
  <li>Add the canned tomatoes with their juice and about 750 ml of water. Stir well and bring to a boil.</li>
  <li>Stir in the macaroni. Lower the heat and simmer, partially covered, for about 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until the macaroni is tender and the sauce has thickened.</li>
  <li>Take the pan off the heat. Stir in half the cheese, scatter the rest over the top, cover the pan and let it stand for about 3 minutes, until the cheese has melted.</li>
  <li>Salt and pepper to taste and serve straight from the pan in deep bowls.</li>
</ol>
