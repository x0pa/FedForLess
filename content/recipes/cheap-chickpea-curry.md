## Why This One Works

Curry at home often stalls at the coconut milk, because one can of it can cost as much as the chickpeas, the spinach and the onion put together. This version lets the tomato sauce do that work instead, with the spices bloomed in oil at the start so the whole pot tastes rounded and rich anyway. Chickpeas and potato make it filling in a way a vegetable curry often is not, the frozen spinach goes in straight from the bag with no washing and no wilting in batches, and rice underneath turns one pot into six full plates. The yield was measured, not guessed: about 2.4 litres of curry spooned over the rice, six plates of roughly 400 ml of curry each.

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Cook the rice in about 600 ml of salted water until tender, about 15 minutes. Fluff it and let it stand covered while the curry cooks.</li>
  <li>Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 5 minutes, until it softens and starts to colour at the edges.</li>
  <li>Add the garlic, curry powder, cumin and turmeric and stir for 1 minute, until the spices coat the onion and smell warm.</li>
  <li>Add the potatoes, the canned tomatoes and about 250 ml of water. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, partially covered, for about 12 minutes, until the potatoes are nearly tender.</li>
  <li>Stir in the chickpeas and simmer for about 5 minutes more, until the potatoes are fully tender and the sauce has thickened a little.</li>
  <li>Stir in the frozen spinach and cook for about 3 minutes, stirring now and then, until it has thawed and folded into the sauce. Salt to taste.</li>
  <li>Rest 5 minutes and serve the curry spooned over the rice in deep bowls.</li>
</ol>
