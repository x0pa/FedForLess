## Why This One Works

Breakfast for a crowd usually means standing at the stove cooking eggs in rounds while the first plates go cold. This bake moves the whole job into the oven. The potatoes roast first until tender with crisp edges, the eggs and milk go over them in one pour, and the cheese melts into a golden top while you make coffee and set the table. One dish feeds six people for a batch cost of $7.61, and the leftovers reheat into packed breakfasts for the next two mornings. The dish cuts into six generous squares, each one a full breakfast of potato, egg and cheese with no side dish required.

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Heat the oven to 180 C (350 F). Grease a large baking dish with a little of the oil.</li>
  <li>Put the potato cubes and the chopped onion in the dish. Drizzle with the rest of the oil, scatter over half the salt and pepper and all the paprika, and toss well so every piece is coated. Spread the potatoes out in one even layer.</li>
  <li>Bake for about 25 minutes, stirring once halfway, until the potatoes are tender when pressed with a fork and starting to colour at the edges.</li>
  <li>While the potatoes bake, whisk the eggs and the milk in a large bowl with the rest of the salt and pepper until well combined.</li>
  <li>Take the dish out of the oven. Pour the egg mixture evenly over the hot potatoes and scatter the grated cheese over the top.</li>
  <li>Bake for about 20 to 25 minutes more, until the eggs are set in the centre and the cheese has melted and turned golden in places.</li>
  <li>Rest for about 10 minutes, then cut into 6 squares and serve warm.</li>
</ol>
