## Why This One Works

Pancakes usually mean a bag of flour, a bottle of syrup and a breakfast that leaves everyone hungry by ten. These take a different route: rolled oats blended into flour at home, eggs and milk for staying power, and apples stewed with cinnamon doing the job syrup does for a fraction of the price of a bottle. The oats are the budget engine, as they are everywhere on this site: the club bag prices them at under three dollars a kilogram, and 400 g of them anchors a breakfast for four people at $5.50 all in. The yield was measured, not guessed: 12 pancakes, three per person, with the apples divided over the top.

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Put the apples in a small pot with the sugar, half the cinnamon and 60 ml of water. Simmer over low heat for about 10 minutes until the apples are soft and the liquid has turned syrupy, then set the pot aside and keep it warm.</li>
  <li>Blitz the oats in a blender or food processor until they look like coarse flour. Tip them into a large bowl with the baking powder, the rest of the cinnamon and the salt.</li>
  <li>Whisk the eggs and milk together in a second bowl, then stir the wet mix into the oats until just combined. Rest the batter for about 5 minutes; it thickens as the oats drink.</li>
  <li>Melt a little of the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Cook ladles of batter for about 2 minutes a side until golden, making 12 pancakes in total and adding butter as the pan needs it.</li>
  <li>Serve three pancakes per person with the warm stewed apples spooned over the top.</li>
</ol>
