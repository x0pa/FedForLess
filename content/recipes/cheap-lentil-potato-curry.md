## Why This One Works

Red lentils are one of the cheapest real proteins in the store, and red lentils in particular ask for almost nothing: no soaking, no long simmer, no separate cooking. They fall apart into the sauce and thicken it as they go, so a pot built on lentils, potatoes and one can of tomatoes eats like something that simmered all afternoon. The potatoes do the stretching work, the rice underneath makes it a full plate, and the whole pot of six servings costs less than a single takeout sandwich. The yield was measured, not guessed: six deep bowls of curry over rice, each one a full meal.

## Ingredients

<ul class="ingredients nums">
  <li><span>Dried red lentils, 400 g</span></li>

  <li><span>Onion, 300 g, finely chopped</span></li>
  <li><span>Canned tomatoes, one 796 ml can</span></li>
  <li><span>White rice, 300 g</span></li>

  <li><span>Curry powder, cumin, turmeric and garlic</span></li>
  <li><span>Salt</span></li>
</ul>

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 5 minutes until softened at the edges.</li>
  <li>Stir in the curry powder, cumin, turmeric and garlic and cook for about 1 minute, until the pot smells warm and spiced.</li>
  <li>Add the lentils, potatoes, canned tomatoes and 900 ml of water. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for about 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils have broken down and the potatoes are tender.</li>
  <li>Meanwhile cook the rice in a second pot according to the pack. Let it stand covered for 5 minutes, then fluff it with a fork.</li>
  <li>Season the curry with salt, simmer for 2 minutes more, and serve it over the rice in deep bowls.</li>
</ol>
