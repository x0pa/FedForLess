## Why This One Works

Sloppy joes were always meant to be generous food for very little money, and lentils suit the job even better than meat does. They cook right in the tomato sauce, soak up the warm spices, and turn tender and saucy with no browning and no draining. The onion and carrot melt into the filling and add a quiet sweetness, the mustard and vinegar keep the rich sauce bright, and a little brown sugar rounds it all out the way a classic sloppy joe should taste. The yield was measured as six buns piled high, each one filling enough to be the meal on its own. The filling tastes even better the next day, and it freezes well for a night when cooking needs to be nothing more than warming a pot and opening a pack of buns.

## Ingredients

<ul class="ingredients nums">
  <li><span>Dried brown lentils, 400 g, rinsed and picked over</span></li>
  <li><span>Diced tomatoes, 1 can (796 ml)</span></li>
  <li><span>Onion, 300 g, finely chopped</span></li>
  <li><span>Carrots, 200 g, finely chopped</span></li>
  <li><span>Hamburger buns, 6, from a pack of 8</span></li>
  <li><span>Canola oil, 2 tablespoons</span></li>
  <li><span>Garlic, 4 cloves, minced</span></li>
  <li><span>Chili powder, 2 tablespoons</span></li>
  <li><span>Brown sugar, 2 tablespoons</span></li>
  <li><span>Yellow mustard, 1 tablespoon</span></li>
  <li><span>White vinegar, 1 tablespoon</span></li>
  <li><span>Smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon</span></li>
  <li><span>Salt, 1 teaspoon</span></li>
  <li><span>Black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon</span></li>
</ul>

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and the carrot and cook for about 6 minutes, stirring now and then, until they have softened.</li>
  <li>Add the garlic, the chili powder and the smoked paprika. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring all the time, until the spices smell warm and toasty.</li>
  <li>Add the lentils and the canned tomatoes with all their juice. Pour in 750 ml of water, stir well, and bring the pot to a gentle boil.</li>
  <li>Lower the heat, cover the pot, and simmer for about 30 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are tender and the sauce has thickened.</li>
  <li>Stir in the brown sugar, the mustard, the vinegar, the salt and the pepper. Simmer uncovered for about 5 minutes more, until the filling is thick enough to pile on a bun, then taste it and add a little more salt if it needs it.</li>
  <li>Spoon the hot filling generously onto the buns and serve at once while everything is steaming.</li>
</ol>
