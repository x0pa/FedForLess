## Why This One Works

A good chili does not need meat to feel complete. Black beans bring the protein and the weight, sweet potato brings a gentle sweetness that suits the warm spices, and corn adds colour and a little pop in every spoonful. The canned tomatoes and their juice become the sauce with no extra work, and the whole pot cooks in one place while the flavours settle together. The yield was measured as six generous bowls, each one filling enough to be the meal on its own. Leftovers taste even better the next day, and the pot freezes well for a week when cooking feels like too much.

## Ingredients

<ul class="ingredients nums">
  <li><span>Black beans, 2 cans (540 ml each), drained and rinsed</span></li>
  <li><span>Sweet potatoes, 900 g, peeled and cut into small cubes</span></li>
  <li><span>Diced tomatoes, 1 can (796 ml)</span></li>
  <li><span>Onion, 300 g, chopped</span></li>
  <li><span>Frozen corn, 300 g</span></li>
  <li><span>Canola oil, 2 tablespoons</span></li>
  <li><span>Garlic, 4 cloves, minced</span></li>
  <li><span>Chili powder, 2 tablespoons</span></li>
  <li><span>Ground cumin, 2 teaspoons</span></li>
  <li><span>Smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon</span></li>
  <li><span>Salt, 1 teaspoon</span></li>
  <li><span>Black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon</span></li>
</ul>

## Method

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until it has softened.</li>
  <li>Add the garlic, the chili powder, the cumin and the smoked paprika. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring all the time, until the spices smell warm and toasty.</li>
  <li>Add the sweet potato cubes and the canned tomatoes with all their juice. Pour in 250 ml of water, stir well, and bring the pot to a gentle boil.</li>
  <li>Lower the heat, cover the pot, and simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the sweet potato is tender when pressed with a fork.</li>
  <li>Add the black beans, the frozen corn, the salt and the pepper. Stir well and simmer uncovered for about 10 minutes more, until the chili has thickened a little and the corn is tender.</li>
  <li>Taste the chili and add a little more salt if it needs it. Serve in deep bowls while it is steaming hot.</li>
</ol>
