---
title: "Basket Weeks"
description: "Cheap weekly meal plans built on one shared basket: several recipes, one shop, and the combined basket price shown up front."
---

Most budget recipes quietly assume you already own half the ingredients. A Basket Week starts from the other end. Several recipes get planned around the same bags and bottles, you buy one basket, and the combined price of that single shop is printed before you cook anything. No recipe on its own page hides its own basket cost either. The pairings share the shopping, and the math is shown both ways so you can check it.
