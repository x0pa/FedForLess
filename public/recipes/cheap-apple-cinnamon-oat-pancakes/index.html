Cheap Apple Cinnamon Oat Pancakes

A cheap pancake breakfast built on blended rolled oats, eggs and milk, topped with cinnamon stewed apples, costed on Canadian prices at about $1.38 a serving for three pancakes each.

Why This One Works

Pancakes usually mean a bag of flour, a bottle of syrup and a breakfast that leaves everyone hungry by ten. These take a different route: rolled oats blended into flour at home, eggs and milk for staying power, and apples stewed with cinnamon doing the job syrup does for a fraction of the price of a bottle. The oats are the budget engine, as they are everywhere on this site: the club bag prices them at under three dollars a kilogram, and 400 g of them anchors a breakfast for four people at $5.50 all in. The yield was measured, not guessed: 12 pancakes, three per person, with the apples divided over the top.

Ingredients Serves 4

  • Rolled oats, 400 g
  • Apples, 300 g, cut into small pieces
  • Eggs, 3
  • Milk, 250 ml
  • Butter, 30 g
  • Sugar, 50 g
  • Cinnamon and baking powder
  • Salt

Method

  1. Put the apples in a small pot with the sugar, half the cinnamon and 60 ml of water. Simmer over low heat for about 10 minutes until the apples are soft and the liquid has turned syrupy, then set the pot aside and keep it warm.
  2. Blitz the oats in a blender or food processor until they look like coarse flour. Tip them into a large bowl with the baking powder, the rest of the cinnamon and the salt.
  3. Whisk the eggs and milk together in a second bowl, then stir the wet mix into the oats until just combined. Rest the batter for about 5 minutes; it thickens as the oats drink.
  4. Melt a little of the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Cook ladles of batter for about 2 minutes a side until golden, making 12 pancakes in total and adding butter as the pan needs it.
  5. Serve three pancakes per person with the warm stewed apples spooned over the top.
Still Hungry?

A spoonful of peanut butter spread on each plate adds about 25 cents a serving on average prices and turns breakfast into something that holds until lunch. The pancakes stand on their own; the peanut butter is for slow weekend mornings.

Questions

How much do these oat pancakes cost per serving?

About $1.38 a serving, for a batch of four servings costing $5.50 in ingredients. Most ingredients are priced on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, and the rolled oats are priced from chain catalogue checks because the national table does not list the club bag. The basket cost, buying every package from an empty pantry, is $30.77. Depending on your province and store, a serving typically lands between $1.22 and $1.55, which puts it in Tier 1.

Do I need a blender to make the oat flour?

No. A blender or food processor makes it in seconds, but the pancakes still work without one: rest the rolled oats in the milk for about 20 minutes to soften, then whisk the batter hard. The pancakes come out a little heartier in texture, which suits them fine.

How filling is a serving, and how do leftovers keep?

A serving is three pancakes with the stewed apples on top, at an estimate of around 480 calories with about 14 g of protein and 9 g of fibre, because the oats stay whole grain whether they are blended or not. Leftover pancakes keep for up to 3 days in the fridge and come back well in a toaster or a dry pan. The stewed apples keep for 4 days and reheat in a minute.
