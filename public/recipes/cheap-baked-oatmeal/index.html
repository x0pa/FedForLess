Cheap Baked Oatmeal with Apple and Peanut Butter

Make Ahead · 50 Min · Tier 1 · Serves 8

Cheap baked oatmeal that turns one baking dish into eight warm breakfasts at about $0.94 a serving, costed on Canadian average prices with the oats confirmed against a chain catalogue check.

Why This One Works

Breakfast is where tight budgets quietly leak money, one rushed purchase at a time. This dish fixes that for a week in a single bake: oats for staying power, peanut butter and egg for protein, and chopped apple baked right in so every square tastes sweet without much sugar. It slices cleanly, travels well, and reheats like a dream, which makes it the rare cheap breakfast people actually look forward to eating four days in a row.

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats, 270 gabout $0.78
  • Peanut butter, 250 gabout $1.49
  • Apples, 400 g, choppedabout $2.59
  • Eggs, 3about $1.21
  • Milk, 375 mlabout $0.66
  • Sugar, 100 gabout $0.14
  • Butter, 30 gabout $0.38
  • Cinnamon, vanilla, baking powderabout $0.30

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 C (350 F). Grease a medium baking dish with a little butter.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, peanut butter, sugar and vanilla until smooth. The peanut butter takes a minute of whisking to let go and blend in.
  3. Fold in the oats, chopped apple, cinnamon, baking powder, salt and the melted butter. Stir until everything is evenly mixed.
  4. Scrape the mixture into the dish and spread it level.
  5. Bake for about 35 to 40 minutes, until the centre feels set when pressed and the edges have pulled slightly away from the dish.
  6. Cool for about 10 minutes, then slice into 8 squares. Serve warm, or cool completely and store covered in the fridge for up to 4 days.
Still Hungry?

A square warmed in a bowl with a splash of hot milk over it eats like a pudding and adds about 10 cents a serving on average prices. A spoonful of yogurt on top does the same job nicely.

Questions

How much does this baked oatmeal cost per serving?

About 94 cents a serving on Canadian average prices checked for August 2026, with the oats confirmed against a No Frills catalogue check in October 2026. The whole dish costs $7.55 in ingredients and cuts into eight squares, with most regions landing between 85 cents and $1.20 a serving.

How long does baked oatmeal keep?

About 4 days in the fridge, covered. Warm a square in the microwave for about a minute and it comes back soft. It also freezes well for up to 2 months, wrapped square by square.

Can I make baked oatmeal the night before?

Yes. Assemble the whole dish, cover it and put it in the fridge unbaked. In the morning let it sit on the counter while the oven heats, then bake as written, adding about 5 minutes.
