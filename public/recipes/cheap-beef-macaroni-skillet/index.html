Cheap Beef and Macaroni Skillet

One Pan · 45 Min · Tier 3 · Serves 6

A cheap beef and macaroni skillet built on ground beef and elbow macaroni simmered in tomato sauce with cheese melted over the top, costed on Canadian average prices at about $2.70 a serving.

Why This One Works

The boxed skillet dinners promise this exact plate and charge for the box, the seasoning packet and the name. This version buys the parts instead: ground beef browned hard for flavour, macaroni that cooks right in the tomato sauce and drinks it up as it goes, and real cheese melted over the top at the end. It lands in Tier 3 because beef and cheese are the two priciest lines in the pan, and the page says so plainly rather than shrinking the portions to chase a lower tier. The yield was measured, not guessed: about 2.4 litres of skillet, six plates of roughly 400 ml each.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef, 500 gabout $8.38
  • Elbow macaroni, 300 gabout $2.02
  • Canned tomatoes, 796 mlabout $2.18
  • Onions, 300 gabout $1.01
  • Cheddar cheese, 150 g, gratedabout $2.12
  • Canola oil, garlic, paprika and oreganoabout $0.46

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef and the onion and cook for about 8 minutes, breaking the beef up with a spoon, until the beef is well browned and the onion has softened. Drain off any extra fat if the beef gave up a lot.
  2. Add the garlic, paprika and oregano and stir for 1 minute, until the spices smell warm.
  3. Add the canned tomatoes with their juice and about 750 ml of water. Stir well and bring to a boil.
  4. Stir in the macaroni. Lower the heat and simmer, partially covered, for about 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until the macaroni is tender and the sauce has thickened.
  5. Take the pan off the heat. Stir in half the cheese, scatter the rest over the top, cover the pan and let it stand for about 3 minutes, until the cheese has melted.
  6. Salt and pepper to taste and serve straight from the pan in deep bowls.
Still Hungry?

A slice of buttered bread adds about 15 cents a serving and catches the last of the sauce. The skillet is the meal on its own; the bread is for the pan, and for anyone who wants the sauce to stretch to the last spoonful.

Questions

How much does this beef and macaroni skillet cost per serving?

About $2.70 a serving on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, for a skillet of six servings costing $16.17 in ingredients. The basket cost, buying every package from an empty pantry, is $36.48. Depending on your province and store, a serving typically lands between $2.45 and $2.95, which puts it in Tier 3.

Why does this one land in Tier 3?

Ground beef sits at $16.75 a kilogram on August 2026 national averages and block cheese at $7.05 for 500 g, so the two main lines in the pan cost more than the beans, lentils or pork behind most recipes here. The honest fix was the label, not a smaller plate: each serving is still about 400 ml of skillet, at an estimate of around 550 calories with about 29 g of protein.

Does this skillet freeze well?

Well enough, for about 2 months. The macaroni softens a little in the freezer, so cool the skillet fast, pack it flat in containers, and reheat it gently with a splash of water until it is steaming again. Cooked fresh it is at its best on the day, and the leftovers keep about 3 days in the fridge.
