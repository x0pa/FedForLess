Cheap Chicken Leg and Rice Bake

Chicken drumsticks roasted right on top of the rice, so the rice cooks in the drippings and every grain earns its keep, costed on Canadian average prices at about $2.44 a serving.

Why This One Works

Chicken legs are the cheapest cut in the case that still feels like a roast dinner, and baking them on top of the rice is the trick that makes this one taste richer than it costs. The rice cooks in the drippings and the vegetable juices instead of plain water, so every grain carries flavour. The vegetables go in straight from the freezer, and the only chopping in the whole dish is one onion. One dish goes into the oven and a full dinner for six comes out, with the pan doing all the work in between.

Ingredients Serves 6

  • Chicken drumsticks, 1.2 kg
  • Long grain rice, 400 g
  • Frozen mixed vegetables, 450 g
  • Onion, 200 g
  • Canola oil, garlic, spices and salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190 C (375 F). Pat the drumsticks dry, then rub them all over with salt, pepper, the paprika and the thyme.
  2. Heat the oil in a large oven safe pan or Dutch oven over medium high heat. Sear the drumsticks for about 4 minutes a side, until the skin is well browned. Lift them out onto a plate.
  3. Spoon off all but about a tablespoon of the drippings. Add the onion and cook for about 3 minutes, then stir in the garlic and the rice and cook for 1 minute more, coating every grain in the drippings.
  4. Pour in about 800 ml of water and stir in the frozen vegetables. Settle everything into an even layer.
  5. Nestle the drumsticks back on top, skin side up. Cover with a lid or foil and bake for about 40 minutes, until the rice is tender and the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Rest, covered, for 10 minutes so the rice finishes steaming. Serve straight from the pan.
Still Hungry?

A slice of buttered bread for mopping up the pan juices adds about 15 cents a serving. The bake is built to be the whole meal, rice, vegetables and chicken in one dish, but the juices at the bottom of the pan deserve bread.

Questions

How much does this chicken and rice bake cost per serving?

About $2.44 a serving on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, for a bake of six servings costing $14.62 in ingredients. The basket cost, buying every package from an empty pantry, is $38.56. Depending on your province and store, a serving typically lands between $2.15 and $2.95, and drumsticks go on sale so often that plenty of weeks it lands lower than that.

Can I use chicken thighs instead of drumsticks?

Yes. Bone in, skin on thighs sit on the rice the same way and cook in the same time. Price them by the kilogram the way this recipe prices drumsticks. In most months the two cuts sit close enough in price that the cost per serving barely moves. Keep the total weight near 1.2 kg so the rice ratio stays right.

Will the rice actually cook under the chicken?

Yes, as long as the pan is covered tightly. The rice steams in the water and the juices running off the chicken, and the 10 minute rest at the end is part of the cooking, not a suggestion. If your lid fits loosely, lay a sheet of foil over the pan before the lid goes on.
