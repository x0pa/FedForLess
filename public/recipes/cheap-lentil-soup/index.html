Cheap Lentil Soup with Potato and Carrot

One Pot · 45 Min · Green Tier · Serves 6

A cheap lentil soup that eats like a meal: red lentils, potato and carrot in one pot, costed on Canadian average prices and built to actually fill the bowl.

Why This One Works

Red lentils are the cheapest reliable way to turn a pot of water into dinner. They need no soaking, they cook in about 25 minutes, and they break down into the soup so it tastes like it simmered all afternoon. The potato does the filling work, the carrot brings sweetness against the tomato, and the cumin and paprika keep it from tasting like health food. The yield was measured, not guessed: about 3.2 litres of soup, six bowls of roughly 530 ml each.

Ingredients

  • Red lentils, 450 g (half a 900 g bag)about $1.81
  • Potatoes, 1 kg, from the big bagabout $1.07
  • Carrots, 500 gabout $1.43
  • Onions, 300 gabout $1.01
  • Canned tomatoes, 796 mlabout $2.18
  • Canola oil, garlic, spices, bouillonabout $0.52

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for about 5 minutes, until the onion softens.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and stir for 1 minute, until they smell like dinner.
  3. Add the lentils, potatoes, canned tomatoes, bouillon cubes and about 1.5 litres of water. Bring to a boil.
  4. Lower the heat and simmer, partially covered, for about 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils have broken down and the potatoes are tender. Add a little more water if it gets thicker than you like.
  5. Salt and pepper at the end. Rest 5 minutes and serve in the biggest bowls you own.
Still Hungry?

The cheapest honest add on is a slice of buttered bread for the bowl, about 15 cents a serving on average prices. The soup is built to stand alone, but nobody has ever been sad about bread with it.

Questions

How much does this lentil soup cost per serving?

About $1.34 a serving on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, for a batch of six big bowls costing $8.02 in ingredients. Priced on Manitoba averages the same pot is about $1.52 a serving, and buying loose produce instead of bags can push it toward $2.13, which is why the recipe teaches the bag buys.

Is lentil soup filling enough for dinner?

This one is built to be. Each bowl carries about 75 g of dry lentils plus a generous portion of potato, which works out to roughly 490 calories, 23 g of protein and 13 g of fibre per bowl as an estimate. It is a main course soup, not a starter.

Does this soup freeze well?

Very well. It keeps about 3 months in the freezer and reheats from frozen on the stove with a splash of water. The potatoes soften a little more each time, which most people count as a feature.
