Cheap Tomato and White Bean Pasta

A cheap pasta dinner built on short pasta simmered with white beans, canned tomato and onion, costed on Canadian prices at about $1.81 a serving and hearty enough for seconds.

35 minTotal Time

Why This One Works

Pasta on its own is cheap but light; pasta with two cans of white beans stirred through is dinner. The beans add protein and fibre for $3.40 across the whole pot, and cooking the pasta in the sauce means the sauce costs nothing extra in water, energy or washing up. One can of tomatoes, one onion and a little cheese over the top carry the flavour, and the beans make a bowl of it hold you until morning. The yield was measured, not guessed: six deep bowls, each one a full serving with seconds in the pot for anyone who wants them.

Ingredients Serves 6

  • Elbow macaroni, 450 g
  • Canned white beans, two 540 ml cans
  • Canned tomatoes, one 796 ml can
  • Onions, 300 g, finely chopped
  • Block cheese, 50 g, finely grated
  • Canola oil, 2 tablespoons
  • Garlic, 3 cloves, minced
  • Dried oregano, 1 teaspoon
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 6 minutes until soft and golden at the edges. Stir in the garlic and oregano for the last minute.
  2. Add the canned tomatoes, 500 ml of water and a good pinch of salt, and bring the pot to a boil.
  3. Stir in the pasta, lower the heat to a steady simmer, and cook for about 12 minutes, stirring often, until the pasta is tender and the sauce has thickened around it.
  4. Drain and rinse the beans and stir them through the pasta. Simmer for about 3 minutes more so the beans heat through and drink up some of the sauce.
  5. Rest the pot off the heat for 2 minutes, season to taste, and serve with the grated cheese scattered over each bowl.
Still Hungry?

Stirring 200 g of frozen spinach through at the end adds about $1.50 to the whole pot on average prices and folds a green vegetable into a dinner that was already complete without it.

Questions

How much does this pasta cost per serving?

About $1.81 a serving on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, for a pot of six servings costing $10.85 in ingredients. The basket cost, buying every package from an empty pantry, is $31.50. Depending on your province and store, a serving typically lands between $1.61 and $2.03, which puts it in Tier 2.

Why cook the pasta in the sauce instead of boiling it separately?

Two reasons. The pasta drinks up the tomato as it cooks, so the flavour goes into the pasta instead of sitting beside it, and the starch it releases thickens the sauce without any added flour or cream. It also saves a pot and the energy to boil a second one, which is a small saving that still counts.

How filling is a serving, and how do leftovers keep?

A serving is a deep bowl of pasta and beans, at an estimate of around 560 calories with about 22 g of protein and 13 g of fibre, most of it from the beans. Leftovers keep for up to 3 days in the fridge. The pasta keeps drinking the sauce as it sits, so reheat it gently with a splash of water to bring the sauce back.
