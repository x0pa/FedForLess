Cheap Potato, Egg and Cheese Breakfast Bake

One Dish · 70 Min · Tier 1 · Serves 6

A cheap potato, egg and cheese breakfast bake built on diced potatoes roasted first for flavour, then eggs and milk poured over and cheddar melted on top, costed on Canadian average prices at about $1.27 a serving.

Why This One Works

Breakfast for a crowd usually means standing at the stove cooking eggs in rounds while the first plates go cold. This bake moves the whole job into the oven. The potatoes roast first until tender with crisp edges, the eggs and milk go over them in one pour, and the cheese melts into a golden top while you make coffee and set the table. One dish feeds six people for a batch cost of $7.61, and the leftovers reheat into packed breakfasts for the next two mornings. The dish cuts into six generous squares, each one a full breakfast of potato, egg and cheese with no side dish required.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes, 900 gabout $0.97
  • Eggs, 8about $3.22
  • Cheddar cheese, 150 g, gratedabout $2.12
  • Milk, 250 mlabout $0.44
  • Onion, 150 g, choppedabout $0.51
  • Canola oil, 1 tablespoonabout $0.05
  • Paprika, salt and pepperabout $0.30

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 C (350 F). Grease a large baking dish with a little of the oil.
  2. Put the potato cubes and the chopped onion in the dish. Drizzle with the rest of the oil, scatter over half the salt and pepper and all the paprika, and toss well so every piece is coated. Spread the potatoes out in one even layer.
  3. Bake for about 25 minutes, stirring once halfway, until the potatoes are tender when pressed with a fork and starting to colour at the edges.
  4. While the potatoes bake, whisk the eggs and the milk in a large bowl with the rest of the salt and pepper until well combined.
  5. Take the dish out of the oven. Pour the egg mixture evenly over the hot potatoes and scatter the grated cheese over the top.
  6. Bake for about 20 to 25 minutes more, until the eggs are set in the centre and the cheese has melted and turned golden in places.
  7. Rest for about 10 minutes, then cut into 6 squares and serve warm.
Still Hungry?

A slice of toast on the side adds about 20 cents a serving before the butter and turns one square into a full plate. The bake is the meal on its own; the toast is for slow mornings and for anyone who likes a crisp edge beside the soft eggs.

Questions

How much does this breakfast bake cost per serving?

About $1.27 a serving on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, for a bake of six servings costing $7.61 in ingredients. The basket cost, buying every package from an empty pantry, is $39.24. Depending on your province and store, a serving typically lands between $1.10 and $1.45, which puts it in Tier 1.

Can I make this breakfast bake ahead of time?

Yes. Bake it fully, cool it, cover it and keep it in the fridge for up to 3 days. Warm a square in the oven at 160 C (320 F) for about 15 minutes or in the microwave for about 90 seconds, until it is steaming again. The squares also travel well for breakfasts away from home.

Why do the potatoes bake first on their own?

Raw potatoes take longer to cook than eggs. Giving the potatoes about 25 minutes on their own means they are tender when the eggs go over them, so the eggs stay soft instead of overcooking while everyone waits on the potatoes. Each finished square lands at an estimate of around 360 calories with about 18 g of protein.
