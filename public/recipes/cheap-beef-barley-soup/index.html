Cheap Beef and Barley Soup

A cheap beef and barley soup built on ground beef and pearl barley, costed on Canadian average prices and made to fill eight deep bowls at about $2.03 a serving.

8Servings
1 hr 10 minTotal Time
$2.03Cost Per Serving

Why This One Works

Beef and barley soup usually means stewing beef, and stewing beef is where the budget goes to die. This version builds the pot on ground beef instead, the cheapest beef on the national average table, browned hard so the soup still tastes like it simmered all day. The barley does the heavy lifting in the bowl: it swells as it cooks, thickens the broth, and keeps a bowl filling for hours. The yield was measured, not guessed: about 4 litres of soup, eight bowls of roughly 500 ml each.

Ingredients Serves 8

  • Ground beef, 500 g
  • Pearl barley, 300 g
  • Potatoes, 500 g, from the big bag
  • Carrots, 400 g
  • Onions, 300 g
  • Celery, 2 stalks
  • Canned tomatoes, 796 ml
  • Canola oil, garlic, thyme, bay and bouillon

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the ground beef and the onion and cook for about 8 minutes, breaking the beef up with a spoon, until the beef is well browned and the onion has softened. Drain off any extra fat if the beef gave up a lot.
  2. Add the garlic, thyme and bay leaves and stir for 1 minute, until the garlic smells warm.
  3. Add the barley, the canned tomatoes, the bouillon cubes and about 2 litres of water. Bring to a boil.
  4. Stir in the potatoes, carrots and celery. Lower the heat and simmer, partially covered, for about 40 minutes, stirring now and then, until the barley is tender and chewy and the vegetables are soft.
  5. Fish out the bay leaves. Salt and pepper to taste, rest 5 minutes, and serve in deep bowls.
Still Hungry?

A thick slice of buttered bread adds about 15 cents a serving and is made for the last of the broth. The soup is the meal on its own; the bread is for the bowl, and for anyone who wants seconds without a second bowl.

Questions

How much does this beef and barley soup cost per serving?

About $2.03 a serving on Statistics Canada average retail prices for August 2026, for a pot of eight bowls costing $16.26 in ingredients. The basket cost, buying every package from an empty pantry, is $39.99. Depending on your province and store, a serving typically lands between $1.85 and $2.30, which keeps it in the Tier 2.

Why ground beef instead of stewing beef?

Ground beef is the cheapest beef in the Statistics Canada table at $16.75 a kilogram on August 2026 averages, well under the stewing cuts, and browning it well gives the soup the deep beef flavour that a long simmer usually builds. There is also no trimming to do and no tough pieces to wait on.

Does this soup freeze well?

Very well, for about 3 months. The barley keeps drinking up the broth as the soup sits, so add a splash of water when you reheat it and the bowl comes back as soup instead of stew. Portion it before freezing and the busy weeknight dinners are handled.
