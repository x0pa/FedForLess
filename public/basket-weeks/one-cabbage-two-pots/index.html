A Week of Eating

01

First Cook

Cook the cabbage roll skillet first, while the pork and cabbage are at their best. It makes six servings, so dinner tonight and lunch tomorrow are already handled.

02

Later in the Week

Cook the perogies with cabbage and onion a few days later. The second cabbage head holds happily in the fridge, and the perogies come straight from the freezer.

For two adults, 12 servings covers most of a week of dinners with lunches left over. Keep cooked food in the fridge for up to four days. The skillet freezes well in portions; the fried perogies are best eaten the day they are cooked.

Questions

Do I have to cook both dishes in the same week?

No. The frozen perogies keep for months, and the rice, oil and onion bag keep too. The pork and the cabbage set the pace: cook the skillet inside the first week, or freeze the pork for later.

Why is the saving smaller than the soup basket?

Because these two recipes share less. The three soups shared almost every bag on the list. This pair shares the onion bag and the cabbage buying, so the saving is real but modest, and it is printed as it is.

Can I swap one of the dishes for something else?

Yes, if the swap shares the bags. Any recipe that draws on the cabbage, onion or rice bags keeps the point of the basket. The pair box on each recipe page lists its partners.
