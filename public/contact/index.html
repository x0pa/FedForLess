Three Things We Want to Hear About

01

A Price That Looks Wrong

Tell us the recipe, the figure, and what you paid or saw, along with the store and city if you're comfortable sharing. Corrections get verified, and when a figure changes, the recipe carries a note about it.

Report a Price →

02

A Recipe You'd Love to See

A dish you need to be cheaper and still filling is exactly the assignment this site exists for. Tell us the meal you'd like to see priced and published.

A swap, a package size, a seasonal trick. If it holds up, we'll test the math and credit the idea in the update note.
