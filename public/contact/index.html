The fastest way to reach us is email: hello@fedforless.ca

Three things we especially want to hear:

  • A price that looks wrong. Tell us the recipe, the figure, and what you paid or saw, with the store and city if you are comfortable sharing them. Corrections get verified and noted on the recipe.
  • A recipe request. A dish you need to be cheaper and still filling is exactly the assignment this site exists for.
  • A cheaper way to make something here. A swap, a package size, a seasonal trick. If it holds up, we will test the math and credit the idea in the update note.
