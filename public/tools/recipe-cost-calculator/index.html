Two Costs. Two Different Numbers.

Ingredient Cost

The proportional cost of what the recipe actually uses. Half a package counts as half its price.

Basket Cost

The amount required to buy full packages at the store, even when the recipe uses only part of each one.

A 900 g package costing $4.00: use 450 g and the ingredient cost is $2.00, but the purchase cost is the full $4.00.
