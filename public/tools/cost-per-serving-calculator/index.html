The Math Is Simple.

Batch Cost ÷ Servings = Cost Per Serving

$11.52 divided by 6 real servings equals $1.92 per serving.

Price each ingredient by the amount used: the package price divided by the package size, times the amount the recipe takes. If you use half a package, count half of its price, not the full package purchase cost. Add a small allowance for spices, oil, salt, and garlic rather than pretending they are free. That total is the batch cost, and the calculator above does the rest.
